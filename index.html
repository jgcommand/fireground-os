02 / Live operations
Dispatch & radio
CH 1 · IDLE
Dispatch communications appear here after the scenario begins. Use the preset commands or transmit your own message below.
Speak naturally: the system infers task, destination, and objective from fireground phrasing. After a verified IC1 CAN, Battalion may assume command by saying “I’ll take it from out here” without a fixed script. Example buttons are optional. IC1’s own hose-line / search IAP assigns E1 and takes that crew inside. Ladder roof-report orders count as assignments. Review the SIMULATION intent entry to check what was understood.
Radio identities: E1 apparatus / C1 captain / E1 operator / E1 crew are separate. C1 uses the incident Command designation while holding command, then returns to C1 after transfer. Other companies respond through their officers. Exterior roof reports may involve ground-ladder placement.
Typed transmissions and simulated unit replies are spoken through your device when Audio is on. Enable sound and press Transmit. Press Check mic to verify the computer’s input device and access permission. Voice-to-text additionally requires browser speech recognition (which may use an online service). If unavailable, use system keyboard dictation in the text field.
